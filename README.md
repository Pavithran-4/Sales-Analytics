## 📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard

The Sales Analytics Dashboard is designed to provide a consolidated view of sales performance, helping users understand important business metrics without having to examine raw data manually.

Through KPI summaries, charts, and visual comparisons, a sales dashboard can help decision-makers monitor performance, identify patterns, and investigate areas that require attention.

### 🔑 Key Dashboard Components

- **Sales Performance:** Monitor overall sales and evaluate business performance over a selected period.
- **KPI Cards:** Display key metrics such as total sales, total profit, order count, and average order value, where supported by the dataset.
- **Product Performance:** Compare products to identify high-performing and underperforming items.
- **Category Analysis:** Understand how different product categories contribute to overall sales.
- **Sales Trends:** Examine changes in sales over time to identify growth patterns or potential declines.
- **Interactive Filtering:** Use available filters or slicers to explore specific periods, categories, products, or other dimensions.
- **Business Insights:** Use the visual findings to identify potential opportunities and areas for further investigation.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

**Data Preparation → Data Cleaning → Data Analysis → KPI Evaluation → Dashboard Development → Business Insights**

1. **Data Preparation:** Review the dataset and understand its structure.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Address missing values, inconsistent formats, and data quality issues where applicable.
3. **Data Analysis:** Analyze sales performance using relevant formulas, queries, or analytical techniques.
4. **KPI Evaluation:** Calculate and evaluate relevant business performance metrics.
5. **Dashboard Development:** Present important metrics and comparisons using suitable visualizations.
6. **Business Insights:** Interpret the results and identify potential opportunities for improvement.
